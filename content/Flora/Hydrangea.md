## Employment / Education
Hydrangea is a forensic botanist. When people die, they sprout flowers. Hydrangea fills in for Albi sometimes to make the person presentable with the flowers they bloomed at death. If necessary, it also takes samples to ascertain cause of death. Outside of forensic work, Hydrangea is also a pathologist consultant for the hospital.

# Places
## Ataraxia
Ataraxia is a city built at the edge of a plateau. The part built on top of the plateau, Upper Ataraxia, has buildings built on buildings with skybridges and railways connecting them. Morpheus has its headquarters near the center of Upper Ataraxia. A canopy was built on top of Lower Ataraxia to maximize surface area for solar energy. 

Lower Ataraxia is situated at the foot of the plateau. People migrated upwards for work, convenience, and the technology Morpheus offered. Eventually Lower Ataraxia became a commuter town, especially after the canopy covered the sun. The foot of the plateau extends towards a desert and eventually the sea. Lower Ataraxia provides hover bikes that glide on the sand to move from the town to the sea. They have hook attachments like trucks to attach boats and other cargo behind them.

## Diverton
Diverton is the old port town where people first settled before migrating across the desert to Ataraxia. Tourism thrives here, both through history and ocean experiences. Diverton primarily exports seafood to Ataraxia via hover bike.

However, due to the water line rising, Diverton is slowly being flooded and many historical sites are now underwater.

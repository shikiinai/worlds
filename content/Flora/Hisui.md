# Personality
Hisui is a quiet sprout. They're a little timid, but they have the most precious smile. They like making things in the sand.

## Trivia
- They have cold hands
- Their skin turns transparent when they get wet
- Their name is typically written as "Jade", but because skeleton flowers look like ice, the first kanji was changed to use the "Ice" kanji instead

# Relationships
## Ayame
They are classmates in Petal 3. Ayame is Hisui's first friend, and they play together. Hisui admires how bright Ayame shines, even next to the Flower Deity.

## The Flower Deity
He is a little scared of the Flower Deity, but they gave him the raincoat and he has never been happier.

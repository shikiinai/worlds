## Employment / Education
Soren was part of the Knights' Outreach program that recruited promising children from Lower Ataraxia into a harsh bootcamp to eventually become Knights.

As a Knight, he answers to Scarlet, but he is embedded into the Keres as a bodyguard after the Diverton incident. As the new kid on the block, he has a lot to prove and just as much to lose.
## Criminal Record
Numerous property damage warnings where he blew up various rooms with his compounds.
## Awards / Achievements
Graduated top of his class in the Outreach program
## Medical Record
<!-- Surgeries, Cavities, Illnesses -->
Numerous treatment records and hospitalizations for poisoning. The victim says only some of them were self-inflicted and all of them accidental.
## Markings
<!-- Scars, Burns, Skin Damage, Birthmarks, Tattoos -->
Occasionally one might see a bandage on his left forearm. He claims a different accident every time.

> [!warning]- Spoilers
> Later, he has two long black scars stretching from his left cheek down his neck, with the farther one cutting right under his left ear. A chunk of his left ear is also missing.

## Other Distinctive Characteristics
<!-- Nose & Eye Shape, Posture, Body Shape, Legs, Moles -->
His mouth seems permanently stuck in a smirk when it's not open, and he has sharp eyes. He wears minimal hoop earrings that closely adhere to his earlobes.
# Personality
<!-- Cheerful / Gloomy? 
Humorous / Violent? Active? Sociable? Intellectual? Virtuous? Expressive? Weakness / Worries / Unusual Traits. What is distinctive about them? -->
Soren is a clown. He's a silly guy who calls himself an "alchemist",  but he's actually using organic chemistry to make his own compounds. He is extremely competitive and believes the end justifies the means, especially when the means include throwing vials at people.
## Formative Experiences
<!-- as an Infant / Young Child; include who was involved -->
The intense competition and unforgiving aptitude tests in the Outreach program molded him into the silly, competitive alchemist he is today. He also believes fair competition has been soured by cheaters and freeloaders.
## Dreams for the Future
To be free of judgment.
## Fears
To never escape judgment.
## Hobbies
<!-- Recreations, Clothing, Shelter, Habits, Favourite Phrases -->
Soren enjoys concocting new compounds and testing their effects. He also spends a significant amount of time, energy, and finances to skincare and beauty products.
## Special Skills
<!-- Fighting Styles / Sports / Dance / Martial Arts / Guns / Driving / Languages -->
Soren's potions range from being completely harmless to excruciatingly painful. His preferred combat style is running away and throwing vials in every direction, confusing everyone else and causing chaos. When forced into close quarter combat, he uses dual knives. 

> [!warning]- Spoilers
> As a trained assassin, his weapons of choice are scalpels, knives and needles. He still uses his potions, but with much more precision and finesse.

## Strengths
If there is any form of competition involved, Soren is guaranteed to place near, if not the top. As the end justifies the means, the path Soren takes to get where he wants to often surprises others.
## Weaknesses
The only competition Soren is guaranteed to lose at is being quiet. His fashion sense is atrocious, but he believes he is simply ahead of his time.

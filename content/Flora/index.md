> Plants who live in the Flora Kingdom, where all plants are classified as angiosperms.

Collection of plant gijinkas / personifications that have found a home in [[Friends#Ren|Ren]]'s world. They have no stories yet.
# Characters
## Morgue
When flora die, their physical body sprouts flowers related to the cause of death and their spiritual essence returns to the Flower Deity to be reincarnated. The body is held in the morgue and arranged for the funeral. As death is temporary to flora, the funeral is a ceremony to remember and celebrate the current life of the person. It feels closer to a party than a vigil. 

After the funeral, the body is returned to the earth and the mausoleum keeper adds a record to the flora's book. The mausoleum does not hold bodies, though it is connected to the morgue. Instead, it is more similar to an archive of books recording the lives and deaths of flora. Albi says it's a building that houses *tomes*.

As of now, it is still unknown why flora in the kingdom die, as they are technically spirits and do not have physical forms.

## Pharmacy
Spiritual illnesses are still illnesses, although for flora they can manifest in the usual manner as physical or mental illnesses. The local pharmacy on this side of town is more of a traditional one, with herbs hanging outside and clay pots of mystery roots sitting next to the doorstep. A flora would hand the pharmacist their medicine tag, some doctor shorthand written on a block or paper depending on the doctor, and the pharmacist would hand you a mysterious bag of powder after a while.

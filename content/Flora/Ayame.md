Precious ball of sunshine, but shy towards strangers. Too young to really understand why everyone is shocked to see it hold hands with the Flower Deity.

## Trivia

# Relationships
## [[Albi]]
Albi occasionally looks after Ayame while the other two siblings are out. Ayame likes Albi because Albi teaches it big words, like cadaver and disposition.

## Chrys
Chrys is the middle sibling, but still older than Ayame. Ayame likes Chrys, because Chrys takes Ayame to the playground to punch things and play on the swing!

## Coco
Coco is the oldest sibling. Ayame doesn't see Coco much, but Coco teaches Ayame a lot of fun dances.

## Ren (Flower Deity)
Ren dotes on Ayame like an oya-baka, holding hands with it and walking it to school every day. Ayame thinks Ren is the best thing in the world and wants to grow up to be like him. Ren needs to resist the urge to pat Ayame's head every time he sees it.

## [[Hisui]]
Hisui and Ayame are friends and classmates! Hisui knows where all the secret sand people live and tells Ayame amazing stories about them.
